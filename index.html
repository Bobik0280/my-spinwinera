    Guest Questions

    The roll must land strictly below your target. If your target is 50, then 1 to 49 count as wins and 50 or higher loses the stake.

    2. How is the payout multiplier set?

    It is based on the true odds of your target, with a small house margin taken off. Lower targets mean fewer winning numbers and therefore a larger multiplier.

    3. Is there a best target to choose?

    No setting changes the long-term return. Higher targets usually mean more frequent smaller wins, while lower targets trade frequency for bigger payouts. Pick the one that suits your approach.
